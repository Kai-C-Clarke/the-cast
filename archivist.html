Alf Kirby — Honorary Archivist

Aye, come in then. Mind the drawings on that bench — some of those are originals. Ask me owt you like about wood, fabric, adhesives, airworthiness, construction — if it's in t'archive I'll find it. And aye — I speak English, Deutsch, Français and Español. If it isn't, I'll tell you straight.
